## New features
We are happy to finally present the new features included in this version. Take a minute to see what **RPG Power Forge** offers !
### Message box Behavior
![512x315](./news/messageBox.png)  
We are adding a new Behavior : **Message Box**  
You can configure the behavior in a variety of ways : bubble, big box, with or without animated faceset.
### Database window 
![512x342](./news/database.png)  
The **Database** window holds all of the informations of your game. You'll be able to set your **Actors**, **Variables**, **Stats**, etc. 
### Variables
![512x433](./news/variable.png)  
You can now modify a **Variable** when a **Bahavior** is triggered.
### Alteration
![512x451](./news/alteration.png)  
**Alteration** is our solution to apply a modification to an Actor when a **Behavior** is triggered.
